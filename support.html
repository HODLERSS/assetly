What Assetly does

It tracks what you own across accounts and writes you a short brief on it: what moved, what it means, and what to watch. You can read it in about two minutes or listen in about ninety seconds.

Common questions

Can it trade?No. Brokerage connections are read-only. Assetly cannot buy, sell, or move money.
Where do prices come from?Public market data, refreshed every minute during market hours.
My brokerage will not connectDisconnect in Settings and reconnect. If it still fails, email the broker name and roughly when you tried.
Audio stops when I lock the phoneIt should keep playing with controls on the lock screen. If it stops, please report your iOS version.
Numbers look wrongEmail the holding and what you expected. Figures are the thing worth getting right.
Delete my accountEmail and it will be deleted, including any brokerage connection and stored audio.

Appearance

Settings has System, Light and Dark. System follows your phone.
