**SpaceX Uncertainty Map**
- The AI segment mixes Grok, X advertising/subscriptions and cloud contracts; the 10-Q does not split out Grok revenue, so "you own Grok" means you own the segment that contains it.
- The $18.37B / $15.83B capex split comes from press reports of the results, not a line we read in the 10-Q (the 10-Q gives the six-month total, consistent with $10.1B in Q1 + $18.4B in Q2).
- 10 GW and Grok 5 are management targets; Musk's timelines have often slipped.
- "Starlink keeps paying for it" is a framing: Connectivity was the only segment with operating profit in Q2; the company also holds about $100B of cash and securities.

## Uncertainty Map (series)

- **Funding inference (Short 2):** Nvidia does not report revenue by these four buyers in our sources; its 10-K lists customer concentration in other terms. "Funding" is a framing, not an accounting link. Capex also buys buildings, power and network gear, not only Nvidia chips.
- **$700B+:** a compilation of company guidance by the FT, not one company figure; individual guidance has moved during the year.
- **Alphabet guidance** comes from the call as reported by two outlets, not the release text itself.
- **Assetly answer** was recorded Oct 2, two days before production; the holdings are illustrative, and the answer's layering is the app's own description.
- **1M views** is a goal, not a forecast. The channel has about 5 subscribers and the Oct 3 explainers drew single-digit views. These Shorts fix hook, pace and topic choice, but reach also depends on distribution, which we do not control.
- What would change the picture: Alphabet's Q3 release (late October) showing free cash flow back above zero or Cloud margins falling; any Big Tech capex cut; Nvidia customer-concentration disclosure in its next 10-Q.
