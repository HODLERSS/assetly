What your money did today, and why.

I'm an Austin-based entrepreneur building Assetly, a free app that puts your stocks, ETFs, crypto and cash in one place and explains each day's moves in plain English. I'd love for you to try it and tell me what to improve, because I read every piece of feedback.

  • Everything you own in one live view
  • A short brief before the open, at midday and after the close
  • Ask questions about your own portfolio
  • Free and read-only: it never trades or moves money
