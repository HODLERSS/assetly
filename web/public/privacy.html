Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 August 2026

Assetly shows you your own portfolio and explains it. It does not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

What is collected

Your emailTo sign you in. Nothing else.
Your holdingsSymbols, quantities, cost basis and account labels, either typed in by you or imported from a brokerage you connect.
Your preferencesBase currency, investor profile, appearance. Stored with your account.

No contacts, no location, no photos, no advertising identifiers, no analytics SDK.

Connecting a brokerage

Brokerage connections are handled by SnapTrade and are read-only. Assetly can see positions and balances. It cannot place trades, move money, or change anything in your brokerage account. Your brokerage credentials are never sent to, or stored by, Assetly: you enter them with SnapTrade or your broker directly. You can disconnect at any time in Settings, which deletes the stored connection.

Who your data is shared with

SnapTradeBrokerage connection and position data, only if you connect an account.
MARA CloudWrites your brief. Receives a summary of your holdings and their figures. Not used to train models.
ElevenLabsTurns the written brief into audio. Receives the narration text, which contains your figures.
SupabaseHosting, database and audio storage.

Market prices and news come from public sources including Yahoo Finance and Google News. Nothing about you is sent to them: those requests ask about a ticker, not about a person.

What is not done

  • Your data is never sold or rented.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.
  • Assetly does not place trades or move money. It is read-only by design.

Keeping and deleting

Your data is kept while your account exists. To delete your account and everything held with it, email the address below and it will be removed, including any brokerage connection and stored audio.

Not financial advice

Assetly describes what you own and what moved. It is information, not investment advice, and not a recommendation to buy or sell anything. Assetly is not a broker, not a financial institution, and not a registered adviser.
