What it does

One live view of everything you own

Connect a brokerage read-only, or add positions by hand. US and Korean stocks, ETFs, crypto and cash, in your base currency.

A brief on what moved, and why

Before the open, at midday and after the close: a few sentences on your biggest movers and the news behind them. Read it or listen to it.

Ask about your own holdings

Questions like "what drove my week?" or "what's my biggest risk?" are answered from your numbers and today's news.

Read-only, always. Assetly can never place a trade or move money. It does not sell your data or show ads. It explains your portfolio; it does not tell you what to buy or sell.
