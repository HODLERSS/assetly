What Assetly is

Assetly is a personal portfolio tracker. It records the holdings you enter or import, prices them with public market data, and writes and narrates a brief that describes what moved and why. It is free to use.

Not financial advice

Every brief, insight, answer and figure in Assetly is information about your own holdings. None of it is investment, tax or legal advice, and none of it is a recommendation to buy, sell or hold anything. Briefs are written by a language model from market data and news and can be wrong, late or incomplete. Check figures against your brokerage before acting on them. You alone are responsible for your investment decisions.

Brokerage connections

Connecting a brokerage goes through SnapTrade and is read-only. Assetly cannot place trades, move money or change anything in your brokerage account. Your brokerage credentials never pass through Assetly. You can disconnect at any time in Settings.

Your account

  • You must be at least 18 to use Assetly.
  • You sign in with Apple, Google, GitHub or an email link. Keep those accounts secure; anyone with access to them can see your holdings.
  • You can delete your account at any time from Settings. Deletion removes your holdings, lots, briefs, insights, narration audio and any brokerage connection, and cannot be undone.
